How sizing works

01

Measure both feet

Stand on paper with your heel against a wall. Mark the end of your longest toe. Measure both feet in your running socks and enter the longer length.

02

Compare official charts

We match measured foot length against each brand’s chart and follow its instructions where available. Between rows, the default starting size is the next larger chart entry. CM and JP are size labels on the shoe, separate from measured foot length.

03

Confirm the model fit

Check the product’s chart, width options and heel hold before buying. On trails, test for toe contact on descents. Foot length alone cannot predict fit or width.
